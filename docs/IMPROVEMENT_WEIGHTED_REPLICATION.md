## 1. Objective

The imbalance ablation (`docs/ICCAD5_IMBALANCE_ABLATION.md`) reported that the
class-weighted recipe (`class_weight={0: 52.73, 1: 0.505}`, the production recipe of
`src/train_xai.py`) is the only condition whose `dense_penultimate` bottleneck collapses to
the exact-zero vector on true-HS test images (85.4%), while `baseline_noweight` and
`balanced_sampling` showed 0.000%. Each condition was trained **once**, at seed 42. The
registry requires a class-weighted replication at seeds **101 / 202 / 303** before any
causal claim about class weighting is admitted. This workstream was asked to (a) audit the
actual provenance, probes, seed control, and checkpoint selection behind the existing
single run, (b) recompute what is recomputable on CPU from stored outputs, and (c) deliver
the exact, testable replication protocol with an honest blocked status.

non-bit-exactness across the three runs).

## 5. Seed control — what is and is not controlled

Verified in source:

- `tf.keras.utils.set_random_seed(seed)` (train_xai.py / both ablation scripts) reseeds
  Python `random`, NumPy global RNG and TF global RNG — this governs weight init and
  `flow_from_directory` shuffling (`src/data.py::data_extractor` passes **no** explicit
  `seed`/`shuffle_seed` to the generator, so it consumes the global RNG state).
- The balanced-sampling generator uses an **independent** `np.random.default_rng(seed)`
  (draw order), threaded with the same seed value.
- Seeds are effective (distinct init checksums across 42/101/202/303 — measured, not
  assumed, in the prior seed-replication work).

Honest limits of seed control, all relevant to interpreting the existing evidence:

1. **The class-weighted recipe has exactly one realized training trajectory ever
   (seed 42).** Its collapse (35/41 HS zero-vector) could be seed-dependent exactly the way
   `baseline_noweight` turned out to be: at seed 42 it showed 0% HS collapse, at 101/202 it
   showed 95.1% / 78.0%. The existing evidence therefore does **not** support "class
   weighting causes the collapse" as a condition-level claim; it supports, at most, "the
   one observed class-weighted run collapsed."
2. Within a fixed seed, TF training is not bit-exact across process runs (documented in
   `ICCAD5_CHECKPOINT_FIX.md` §6): two same-seed runs can differ. So "seed" here controls
   the *draw*, not the *outcome*.
3. The seed-replication families deliberately reuse seeds 101/202 across condition
   families (documented design choice), so the existing non-weighted replications do not
   constitute a fully crossed 3×3 design either.

## 6. Best-checkpoint selection and the "validation" split — selection-on-test confirmed

- `src/data.py::dataset_analysis` names the **test** directory `validation_dir`
  (`validation_dir = base/test`). Every training script passes that generator as
  `validation_data` to `model.fit`.
- `BestBalancedAccuracyCheckpoint` (`src/metrics.py`) then selects and restores the epoch
  with the best **validation** (= test) balanced accuracy, computed at 0.5 threshold from
  native Keras `val_true_positives/...` counters. This audit verified from the stored
  history that the selected epoch (8) is exactly the first argmax of
  `history_val_balanced_accuracy`, and that the final epoch (9) was worse (0.8474).
- Reported "test" metrics are therefore computed on the **same split used for model
  selection** — for every condition and every run in this line of work (baseline, XAI
  training, checkpoint fix, ablation, seed replication). There is no held-out validation
  set anywhere in the pipeline.
- Impact, stated carefully:
  - Reported balanced accuracies are **optimistically biased** (selection-on-test), by an
    amount that grows with per-epoch variance — and ICCAD5's val-bal-acc swings
    0.50→0.96 within single runs.
  - Because the selection set is identical across conditions, this does **not** by itself
    manufacture the ablation's between-condition differences, but it does mean (a) absolute
    numbers must not be compared to literature or other splits, (b) seed-level
    comparisons at n=41 HS are noisier than they look (selection amplifies lucky epochs),
    and (c) the weighted-vs-alternative comparisons inherit this bias **asymmetrically in
    principle** (different conditions have different per-epoch variance).
- This finding affects all prior workstreams' reported metrics equally; it is recorded
  here because H is the workstream whose protocol would otherwise inherit it. §9's
  protocol fixes it for the replication runs while keeping comparability.

## 7. Testable hypothesis (pre-registered before any replication run)

**H1 (primary).** Across independent seeds s ∈ {101, 202, 303}, class-weighted ICCAD5
training (exact production recipe) produces HS zero-vector rates in the
`dense_penultimate` layer **strictly greater** than balanced-sampling training at the same
seeds, with the weighted runs' mean HS zero-vector rate ≥ 50%.

- Falsification of H1 (i.e., no class-weight-specific effect): mean weighted HS zero-vector
  rate is statistically indistinguishable from the balanced-sampling runs at the same
  seeds (Mann-Whitney U over per-seed rates, α = 0.05, n = 3 vs 4 including seed 42 —
  exact test; or, equivalently, overlapping per-seed values with overlapping CIs).
- Confirmation is **descriptive replication**, not proof of mechanism: 3 seeds cannot
  establish a population rate (the `baseline_noweight` family already demonstrates a
  1-of-3-seeds flip from 0% to 78–95% HS collapse).

**H1a (secondary, directional, classification).** Weighted runs' test balanced accuracy at
seeds 101/202/303 will fall below the corresponding balanced-sampling runs' (existing:
0.9731–0.9743) in at least 2 of 3 seeds.

**H1b (secondary, representation).** Weighted runs will produce 0 HS-elevated units
(AUC ≥ 0.90) in at least 2 of 3 seeds.

**Multiple-testing family:** {H1, H1a, H1b} — three tests; any headline claim must survive
Holm correction within this family (α_family = 0.05). Per registry rules these are
exploratory, confirmatory only in the sense of pre-specified direction and criterion.

**Prior probability honesty.** The existing 5-run replication evidence cuts **against**
treating weighting as uniquely causal: the non-weighted `baseline_noweight` family
collapsed on 78–95% of HS images in 2 of its 3 total runs (42/101/202). The honest current
statement is: *collapse on true-HS images is common across recipes (observed in the single
weighted run and 2 of 3 baseline_noweight runs) and consistently absent in the balanced-
sampling family (4 runs, 0%)*. H1 tests whether weighting specifically — not merely
"non-balanced training" — drives it.

## 8. Exact replication protocol (to execute only after GPU unblock + lead approval)

Fixed (byte-identical to the production recipe — do not "improve" anything):

- Data: `iccad-official/iccad5/{train,test}` unmodified; `ImageDataGenerator(rescale=1/255)`,
  `target_size=(224,224)`, `class_mode='binary'`, train shuffle=True, test shuffle=False,
  batch 32. Train counts 26 HS / 2,716 NHS.
- Architecture: `src.model_xai.build_xai_cnn((224,224,3))` (128,321 params, unchanged).
- Optimizer `nadam` (defaults; lr 0.001), loss `binary_crossentropy`, metrics
  `TRAIN_METRICS` exactly as in `src/train_xai.py`; epochs 10; batch 32.
- `class_weight = {0: 2742/(2·26), 1: 2742/(2·2716)}` computed by
  `src.train_xai.compute_class_weights` (values 52.73077…/0.50479…).
- `tf.keras.utils.set_random_seed(seed)` **per run, in a fresh process**, seed ∈ {101, 202,
  303}; standard `data_extractor` train generator (no balanced sampling, no other change).
- Checkpoint selection: `BestBalancedAccuracyCheckpoint`, unchanged.
- Run names: `class_weighted_replication{101,202,303}`; outputs only under
  `results/xai/improvements/weighted_replication/` and checkpoints under
  `models/xai/experimental/` — never touching existing artifacts.

Measurement additions (required, to fix known measurement gaps):

1. **Probes:** per-sample `dense_penultimate` 16-vector for all 19,368 test images per run,
   extracted with the **official** preprocessing path
   (`load_img(..., interpolation='nearest')` → `/255.`, matching
   `ImageDataGenerator`); record per-unit **minimum activation** per sample-set so the
   "exact zero" claim is auditable (the historical CSVs store rounded floats where exact
   zero vs < 1e-7 cannot be distinguished post hoc — see §9).
2. **Selection audit trail:** log per-epoch test (= "validation") balanced accuracy, as the
   existing pipeline does, **and additionally** record final-epoch metrics, so the
   selection-on-test inflation is quantifiable rather than invisible.
3. **Optional guard metric (documented deviation):** additionally record the best epoch by
   *last-10%-of-train* balanced accuracy to bound how much of the result is
   selection-on-test. This does not replace the production criterion (which stays primary
   for comparability); it is recorded alongside.
4. Store per-run `metrics.json` in the same schema as
   `results/xai/iccad5_seed_replication/training/<run>/metrics.json` (adds
   `init_weight_abs_sum_checksum` and `checkpoint_selection` exactly as
   `src/run_iccad5_seed_replication.py` already emits).
5. Noise probes: reuse the fixed 60-image noise protocol
   (`np.random.default_rng(12345)`, 30 uniform + 30 binary p=0.3) for continuity with
   `ICCAD5_SEED_REPLICATION.md` §10.

Analysis: per-seed confusion matrix, balanced accuracy, HS/NHS zero-vector rates by case
type, dead units, per-unit AUC (tie-corrected), centroid/within-NHS ratio; tests per §7.

**Comparability caveat to carry into the analysis (from §2):** the existing seed-42
class-weighted point comes from a *different process run* than the ablation's other
conditions; the replication adds three fresh same-recipe runs, which is exactly what H1
needs, but direct numeric comparison of "class_weighted(42)" vs new runs must note
process-level non-determinism even at equal seeds.

## 9. Honest unknowns / provenance caveats discovered (not resolvable on CPU)

1. **Activation-extraction provenance for the historical CSVs.** The ablation report (§11)
   states the extraction was corrected to `load_img` nearest-neighbor after a bicubic bug,
   and that TP/TN/FP/FN then matched the official counts — which this audit independently
   confirms (stored flags/predictions/counts reproduce exactly). However, **no extraction
   script or manifest for that CSV exists in the repository** (`src/` and `scratch/` were
   searched), so the correction cannot be re-verified from code. Label the stored
   activations: *historical, report-attested nearest-neighbor provenance, not independently
   prediction-validated*.
2. **Exact-zero semantics.** The stored CSVs carry full float32 string representations
   (a tiny activation would print as e.g. ``1e-08``, not ``0.0``), so the exact-zero counts
   recomputed here are faithful to what was stored. The residual gap is upstream of the
   CSV: without the extraction script (item 1) we cannot rule out that the extractor's own
   float handling (e.g., underflow or rounding before the write) differed from a future
   re-extraction. The 0% vs 85% contrast is far beyond any such concern; a future boundary
   case (1–2 zero HS samples) should re-extract with per-unit minima logged.
3. **Float-print risk in the selection criterion** (`0.5*(sens+spec)` with 0/0 → 0.0 for
   empty denominators) is theoretical here (41 HS and 19,327 NHS guarantee non-empty
   denominators) and noted only for completeness.

## 12. Limitations

- All metrics recomputed here describe **stored artifacts**; they inherit any error in
  those artifacts (mitigated but not eliminated by the exact-match checks and §9.1).
- n = 1 weighted run, n = 3/2 replications of the *other* conditions; no per-seed

## 13. Decision framework

- **Q1: Is the collapse established as a class-weighting effect?** No. One weighted run
  collapsed; two of three non-weighted `baseline_noweight` runs also collapsed heavily.
  The only clean replicated contrast is *balanced sampling (4 runs, 0%) vs everything
  else*. H1 is the test that would license the causal attribution.
- **Q2: Is the existing weighted result itself trustworthy?** Its stored metrics are
  internally consistent (recomputed exactly) and its checkpoint is identified and
  checksummed; its absolute numbers are selection-on-test optimistic (§6), as are all
  conditions'.
- **Q3: Should `class_weighted` seeds 101/202/303 run next?** Yes —
  per §8, highest priority within this workstream; they are the single missing piece
  between "one collapsed run" and any claim about the recipe.
- **Q4: May any production or experimental checkpoint change based on this document?** No.
  Nothing is deleted, moved, overwritten, or renamed by this workstream; the ablation's
  own "balanced_sampling selected for continuation" research default is untouched and
  remains conditional on the wider sweep recommended in `ICCAD5_SEED_REPLICATION.md` §13.
- **Q5: Does this audit change prior workstreams' conclusions?** It adds two caveats that
  should accompany any citation of ablation numbers: (i) condition B is the production
  checkpoint-fix run (run-identity caveat, §2); (ii) all metrics are selection-on-test
  (§6). It does not overturn any prior finding on the evidence available.
